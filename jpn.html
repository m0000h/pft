Ebisu Circuit, Nihonmatsu — All 7 tracks · Night drifting · Vendors · Drift taxis · Passenger rides
🕗 7:30AM — Early arrival (beat the queue)
🌅
Arrive Early — Gates Open 8AM
Queues form before 8AM. Arriving early means better parking (priority to drivers) and first access to vendor stalls. Green spectator wristband will be issued. Wear your warmest layers — Fukushima in November is cold from the morning.
🕘 9AM–Midnight+ — THE MATSURI
🏁
Kita (North) Course — Best spectator spot
Cars pass within inches of spectators on the back stretch. Open 9AM–midnight Saturday. Get close, get loud. Bring earplugs if sensitive to noise — these cars are LOUD. Great photo spot.
KITA COURSE
🌙
Night Drifting — After Dark
The Matsuri runs through the night. Night drifting under floodlights is one of the most surreal experiences in motorsport. Brave souls continue past midnight. The cold drops significantly — have your Donki-bought thermals on.
NIGHT DRIFT
🚗
Drift Taxi / Passenger Rides
Ask drivers for a passenger ride — international fans are warmly welcomed. Say 「同乗させてください！」 (Dojou sasete kudasai!) or just gesture enthusiastically. Rent a helmet at the circuit (required). Sideways Experience also offers paid passenger rides. Budget ¥3,000–¥10,000 depending on who you ride with.
Budget: ¥3,000–¥10,000
DRIFT TAXI
🍺
Circuit food, drinks & merchandise stalls
Ramen, yakitori, onigiri, beers, hot drinks. Merch stalls sell Ebisu Circuit gear, team merchandise, rare JDM goods. Budget ¥5,000–¥10,000 across the full day. This is also where you'll find circuit-exclusive items not sold elsewhere.

📋 Key Tips & Reminders

  • 2026 Autumn Matsuri confirmed Nov 14–15 — matches your dates perfectly. Check official Ebisu Circuit website closer to date for any updates.
  • Book mt. inn Dake Onsen NOW — sells out 3–6 months ahead for Matsuri weekends. It's the best reviewed Ebisu hotel and less than 10 min from the circuit.
  • Up Garage is CLOSED every Wednesday — Days 4 and 10 are on Wednesdays, planned accordingly.
  • Bring your passport everywhere — HardOff, BookOff, Surugaya, Donki all offer 10% tax-free over ¥5,000.
  • Get an ETC card with your rental (¥500/day) — discounts on night/weekend expressway driving, no stopping at tolls, saves time and money.
  • Ebisu in November is COLD — buy cheap thermals, gloves and a beanie at Donki early in the trip. November nights in Fukushima drop below 5°C.
  • UFO Catcher tips: Watch 2–3 plays before spending money. Target older prize sections for better odds. Ask staff for an 「アシスト」(assist) if stuck — they'll help position the prize. Budget ¥500–¥1,000 per session.
  • Yamato Transport shipping: Available at any convenience store. Ship boxes to your Australian address before flying. Much cheaper and easier than extra baggage fees.
  • Round1 Stadium — Japan's best entertainment complex. Bowling, karaoke, arcades, batting cages. Budget ¥2,000–¥4,000 for a full evening session.
  • GEO stores are often open until midnight — great for evening gaming or capsule toy runs after dinner when other stores close.
  • Exchange rate: ¥97/AUD used — verify current rates before travel. Carry some cash (¥50,000 minimum) as smaller stores are cash-only.
